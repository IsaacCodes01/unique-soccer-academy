Who we are

Our Story

Unique Soccer Academy is a vibrant third-division football club in Kasoa-Apra, in the Central Region of Ghana. It was founded on 20th March 2020 by Mr. Nii Perrygrino Otoo, and began in Anyaa, Greater Accra, with just 12 passionate players training at Anyaa M/A School.

After two moves, the academy found its current home in Apra on 11th February 2024, where it keeps nurturing the dreams of young athletes.

What drives us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to nurture football talent while creating pathways for our players to reach professional clubs, academies, and scholarship opportunities locally and internationally.

Our Vision

To establish Unique Soccer Academy as a globally respected institution that transforms promising young footballers into professional athletes and well-rounded individuals, setting the gold standard for player development and opportunity creation.
