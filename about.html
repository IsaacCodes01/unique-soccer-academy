Since 2020

Our Story

Unique Soccer Academy is a vibrant third-division football club located in Kasoa-Apra, in the Central Region of Ghana.

It was founded on 20th March 2020 by the visionary Mr. Nii Perrygrino Otoo. The academy began in Anyaa, Greater Accra, with just 12 passionate players training at Anyaa M/A School.

Over the years, the academy relocated twice: first to Auntie-Aku's Kokompe Park, and later to its current home in Apra on 11th February 2024. Despite challenges, its growth has been driven by unwavering commitment and leadership.

The academy was solely managed by Mr. Otoo until he partnered with Mr. Emmanuel Opare Asamoah, CEO of Opas Construction, who became a major sponsor. Together, they continue to guide and nurture the dreams of young athletes.

Today, Unique Soccer Academy proudly trains many dedicated players, advancing its mission to nurture football talent and provide pathways to professional clubs, academies, and scholarship opportunities both locally and internationally.

Our vision is clear: to become a globally respected institution that transforms promising young footballers into professional athletes and well-rounded individuals, setting the gold standard for development and opportunity.
